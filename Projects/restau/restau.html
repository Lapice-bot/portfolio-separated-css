Peking Duck

Main Course • ₱2,999




Peking Duck is one of China's most famous dishes, known for its crispy golden skin and tender, juicy meat. It is roasted until perfectly crisp and is traditionally served with thin pancakes, cucumber, spring onions, and sweet hoisin sauce. The combination of crunchy skin, soft meat, and flavorful toppings creates a rich and balanced taste that is enjoyed around the world.

Kung Pao Chicken

Main Course • ₱499




Kung Pao Chicken is a popular Sichuan dish made with diced chicken, peanuts, dried chili peppers, and vegetables. It has a unique combination of spicy, sweet, savory, and slightly tangy flavors, making every bite flavorful. The dish is stir-fried over high heat to keep the chicken tender while giving the vegetables a fresh and crisp texture.

Sweet and Sour Pork

Main Course • ₱459




Sweet and Sour Pork is a classic Chinese dish made with crispy fried pork coated in a bright sweet and tangy sauce. It is commonly served with colorful bell peppers, onions, and pineapple, which add freshness and texture. The crunchy pork combined with the rich sauce makes it one of the most popular Chinese dishes worldwide.

Mapo Tofu

Main Course • ₱349




Mapo Tofu is a traditional Sichuan dish made with soft tofu cooked in a spicy sauce with minced pork or beef. The sauce is flavored with chili bean paste, garlic, and Sichuan peppercorns, giving it a bold and slightly numbing taste. It is usually served with steamed rice to balance its rich and spicy flavors.

Beef Chow Fun

Main Course • ₱429




Beef Chow Fun is a stir-fried noodle dish made with wide rice noodles, tender slices of beef, bean sprouts, and green onions. The ingredients are cooked over high heat with soy sauce to create a smoky flavor known as "wok hei." Its soft noodles and savory taste make it a favorite comfort food in Chinese cuisine.

Char Siu

Main Course • ₱449




Char Siu is a Cantonese-style barbecued pork that is marinated in a flavorful mixture of honey, soy sauce, five-spice powder, and other seasonings. It is roasted until the outside becomes slightly caramelized while the inside stays juicy and tender. Char Siu is often served with rice, noodles, or steamed buns and is loved for its sweet and savory flavor.
